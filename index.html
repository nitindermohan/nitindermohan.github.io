About

I am a Ph.D. student in Collaborative Networking (CoNe) research group at Department of Computer Science, University of Helsinki, Finland. My Ph.D. studies are being supervised by Prof. Dr. Jussi Kangasharju. My work is also co-supervised by Prof. Dr. Xiaoming Fu (University of Göttingen, Germany) and Dr. Fabian Schneider (NEC Research Lab, Germany).

Major chunk of my research and Ph.D. studies is being funded by the prestigious European Union's Marie-Curie fellowship and I am working under their Initial Training Network (ITN) project CleanSky. I am also part of the Finnish Academy of Sciences funded project Bright Clouds Dark Clouds (BCDC). At University of Helsinki, I am a part of the well known NODES research group which housed the creation of Linux operating system in late 1990's.

NEW: I am now also working on Academy of Finland sponsored Adaptive and Intelligent Data (AIDA) project. This project is a joint collaboration between University of Helsinki and Aalto University in Finland.

Research

I consider myself as a "distributed systems" guy as I have always been involved in various domains (read "avatars") of this field. "Divided we stand, united we fall" is my motto in the context of computer networking research.

Edge Computing
A broad theme of my research explores the physical deployment, specifications and effective utilization of compute resources at the network "edge". The problem is complex primarily due to different requirements imposed by emerging applications. For example, Internet-of-Things need data aggregation, autonomous vehicles require graphical processing, automated industry necessitates task interoperability and AR/VR demands ultra-low network latency. Incorporating such varying application demands to model an always available, semi-managed network of edge servers is something I quite enjoy.
Publications EdgeSys '19   MECOMM '18   AoT '17   MECOMM '17   CIoT '16
Next Generation Network Protocols
Emerging applications have significantly changed the way we interact with the underlying network. Our old-yet-faithful protocol, TCP has started to show cracks in performance, especially in cellular and wireless environments. I enjoy exploring and designing next-generation internet protocols which can plug-in several existing performance holes. For this measure, I am interested in working on protocols such as Multipath TCP (MPTCP), 5G and Information Centric Network (ICN).
Publications Mobicom '18   IFIP Networking '18   CCNxCon '15
Blockchains and Distributed Ledgers
While edge computing has gathered quite a traction in research community, the management and operation of such large semi to fully decentralized compute facilities in real world remains an open question. Blockchains and Distributed Ledgers emerge as a probable technology which can allow edge providers to gain revenue, auction resources and sign service level agreements with application providers. I am currently exploring how this technology can alleviate other open operability issues plaguing real-world deployment of edge clouds.
Publications ICDCS '19   HotEdge '19
Application & Service Deployment
There is a rapid shift in the make-up of cloud computing as cloud providers try to incorporate edge resources and edge accelerators widely distributed in the region. The brunt of the complexity will be borne by existing cloud-based application as they need to depart from traditional monolithic client-server model. Another aim of my research is to devise intelligent softwarized applications and services which can adapt, adjust and adopt to changes in underlying compute infrastructure without sacrificing performance or quality-of-service.
Publications HotNets '18   CrossCloud '18
