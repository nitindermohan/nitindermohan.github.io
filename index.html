About

Me

I am a Ph.D. student in Collaborative Networking (CoNe) research group at Department of Computer Science, University of Helsinki, Finland. My Ph.D. studies are being supervised by Prof. Dr. Jussi Kangasharju. My work is also co-supervised by Prof. Dr. Xiaoming Fu (University of Göttingen, Germany) and Dr. Fabian Schneider (NEC Research Lab, Germany).

Major chunk of my research and Ph.D. studies is being funded by the prestigious European Union's Marie-Curie fellowship and I am working under their Initial Training Network (ITN) project CleanSky. I am also part of the Finnish Academy of Sciences funded project Bright Clouds Dark Clouds (BCDC). At University of Helsinki, I am a part of the well known NODES research group which housed the creation of Linux operating system in late 1990's.

NEW: I am now also working on Academy of Finland sponsored Adaptive and Intelligent Data (AIDA) project. This project is a joint collaboration between University of Helsinki and Aalto University in Finland.

Research

I consider myself as a "distributed systems" guy as I have always been involved in various domains of this field. "Divided we stand, united we fall" is my personal take in the context of computer science.

For my Masters research at IIIT-Delhi, India , I worked on providing a scalable checkpointing mechanism for distributed applications operating on Information Centric Networks (ICN). During my brief stint as Project Scientist at IIT-Delhi, India, I worked in an Intel Labs, USA funded research project which aimed at sharing resources of computationally-insufficient devices in a Device-to-Device (D2D) network.

Majority of my Ph.D. research work involves distributing the current cloud architecture to devices closer to the network "edge". Apart from proposing a radical shift in the current cloud architecture as we know it, I am also looking into several problems regarding edge cloud deployments such as managing data, network, services and ownership [we do end up playing with crypto-currencies as well ;-)].
